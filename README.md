# Herald - Statamic Starter Kit

Herald is a newsroom starter kit. File dated stories, assign beats, name reporters, and publish features. Built from a professional-services kit and rewritten for a city and business desk.

**Live demo:** https://herald-statamic.webbydemo.in/  
**Local demo:** http://192.168.29.45:8019/  
Control Panel: `/cp` — `admin@example.com` / `password`

## Who this is for

- Independent papers and city desks
- Business and trade magazines
- Association newsrooms
- Campus or neighborhood papers

## Pages

- Home
- About the Desk
- Coverage Desks
- Sections
- Features
- News
- Reporters
- Newsroom Jobs
- FAQs
- Contact the Desk

## Collections

- **News** (`our_insights`): dated stories
- **Sections** (`industries`) and beats (`industry_sub_categories`)
- **Features** (`case_studies`): longform
- **Reporters** (`our_consultant`): bylines
- **Coverage Desks** (`our_services`)
- **Newsroom Jobs** and applications
